*Unity project for the game EPIC MOUSE CURSORS!!! | Made in a week for [Jame Gam #62](https://itch.io/jam/jame-gam-62)*

Complete your office workday while surviving glitches caused by a suspiciously “free” EPIC Mouse Cursor Pack! Read emails, update your quarterly report, and race against the clock as unexpected interruptions put your mouse skills to the test.


---

## How to Play

1. **Read your boss's email** and follow the instructions to complete your quarterly report.

2. **Navigate your desktop**, open files, and submit your finished work.

3. **Survive glitch interruptions** by playing fast-paced, mouse-controlled minigames.

4. **Beat the 5 PM deadline** to finish your workday!

> **Note:** Only one minigame is available for now, with more planned for the future.

**Controls:** Mouse only.
